Sepolia auction rehearsal terms

Effective September 3, 2026

Test-only experience

This page governs one synthetic auction rehearsal on Ethereum Sepolia. It is not an offer to sell artwork, and no production asset, legal title, or commercial ownership right is represented.

Bids and identity

An eligible preview member may sign an off-chain bid with a pre-provisioned Safe passkey. The signature is binding only inside this test rehearsal. The marketplace associates the test wallet record with that member’s authenticated preview account without publishing their social identity or wallet address in the public bid feed.

Payment

If Stripe sandbox setup is enabled, Apple Pay or a card may appear through Stripe-hosted Checkout on an eligible device. Sandbox setup must not create a real charge. No test bid authorizes a production charge, and the preview currently makes no claim that winner charging, tax calculation, dispute handling, or payment cure is complete.

NFT and gas

The rehearsal NFT is minted to a platform inventory Safe on Sepolia. The bidder does not need Sepolia ETH to sign an off-chain bid. The preview currently makes no claim that automated NFT delivery or gas-sponsored account provisioning is complete.

No reliance

Sepolia state, test payment data, bids, and preview accounts may be reset or removed. Do not submit real funds, secrets, production credentials, or valuable assets.
