# Launch boundary

The Marketplace & Auction House of Brooklyn concept is based at 29 Nassau Avenue. Its launch target represents every approved marketplace work by an NFT on Ethereum mainnet. This is a target, not a claim that the reference source is approved for live financial use.

## Curator-first go-to-market

Start with invited curators and rights-cleared works, low limits, manual inventory release, clear refund/chargeback handling and a fully reconciled Sepolia rehearsal before seeking production approval.

## Instagram chat intake

Use the official Meta APIs, explicit consent, signed webhooks and a private discovery inbox. There is no scraping and no automatic right to mint someone else's social content. App approval and rights review precede any publication.
